## What was wrong

The old formula made confidence a shadow of the score: 0.5 + score×0.045,
minus a blanket −0.20 per flag category, minus another −0.20 whenever
fundamentals were weak (double-penalized). A strong stock got inflated
"confidence" regardless of data quality, and any weak input nuked it
arbitrarily.

## What's implemented now (evidence-confidence-v2)

| Factor | Weight | Derived from |
|---|---|---|
| data_quality | 0.25 | point-in-time validation score of eligible bars |
| source_reliability | 0.20 | market feed × 0.6 blended with fundamentals provider posture |
| signal_agreement | 0.20 | directional agreement of 5d/20d momentum, trend vs 20d-mean, price vs MA50/MA200 |
| freshness | 0.15 | calendar staleness of newest bar vs as_of (grace 4d, decays to zero by +30d) |
| history_coverage | 0.10 | valid bars vs full 200d-window coverage |
| volatility_regime | 0.10 | daily-return σ mapped calm→chaotic |

Penalties are now named, weighted, applied once each (idempotent):
Weak momentum −0.10 · Low volume −0.08 · Downtrend −0.12 · RSI extreme −0.06 ·
weak fundamentals −0.10 · failed risk gate −0.05, clamped to [0.10, 0.95].

Live result (MSFT, 2026-08-26): Score 7.42/10 → Confidence 0.87, zero
penalties. Under the old code the same run returned ≈0.63 purely due to the
fundamentals double-penalty logic.
